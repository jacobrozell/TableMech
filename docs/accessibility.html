Our commitment

TableMech aims at WCAG 2.1 AA on the Ridge Line path: Play, the battle table, Rules, and Settings.

Built-in iOS accessibility

  • VoiceOver — labels on primary controls, including target numbers, phase, and side.
  • Dynamic Type — text uses system styles and scales with your font size.
  • Touch targets — primary controls are at least 44×44 points.
  • Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency — the app follows those system settings.
  • Dark Mode — System, Light, or Dark in Settings.
  • Color is not the only signal — hits, misses, sides, and phases also use text or symbols.

Known limitations

  • English only.
  • Dense battle layouts may need scrolling at the largest text sizes on a small phone.
  • Support, privacy, and download links open in Safari.

Feedback
