Overview

TableMech is an offline iOS app for playing BattleTech Alpha Strike at the table. You do not need an account. Match data stays on your iPhone or iPad. This build does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.

Data stored on your device

  • Saved games — in-progress Ridge Line matches, including rolls and damage you enter.
  • Getting Started checks — optional checklist marks.
  • Preferences — dice mode, haptics, keep-screen-awake, and theme.

None of that is uploaded. Delete saved games from Settings, or delete the app to remove all of it.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, and no developer-operated cloud profile.
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics or crash reports in this version.
  • No sale of personal information.

Network use

  • Play — Ridge Line works without internet after install.
  • Settings links — Privacy, Support, and Accessibility open in Safari when you tap them.
  • Official downloads — optional links to the BattleTech downloads page open in Safari.

Children

TableMech is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
