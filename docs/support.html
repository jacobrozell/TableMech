Get help

TableMech is an offline companion for a BattleTech Alpha Strike Ridge Line game: initiative, movement, shot results, damage, and criticals on one shared iPhone or iPad. No account required.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email jacob.rozell83@gmail.com with your iOS version, device, and what you expected to happen.

Or open an issue: github.com/jacobrozell/TableMech/issues

Common questions

  • Offline use — Play and Rules work without a network after install.
  • First game — Play tab, then Ridge Line. Bring two dice, a tape, and the four miniatures named on the card.
  • Dice — real dice are the default. Settings can switch a new game to in-app rolls.
  • Saved games — leaving a battle keeps it. Settings → Delete saved games removes matches and Getting Started checks. Theme and dice settings stay.
  • Classic BattleTech — shown as Coming soon. It is not playable in this version.
